# Kilele Solar Ltd: Company Overview

(Kilele Solar Ltd is a fictional company created for testing Teacher Sulemani. None of these products, prices or policies are real.)

## Who we are

Kilele Solar Ltd sells and installs solar power systems for homes and small businesses in Kenya. The company was founded in 2018 and is headquartered in Nakuru, with branches in Nairobi, Eldoret and Kisumu.

## Mission

To give every Kenyan household and small business reliable, affordable power that they own.

## Our customers

1. Rural and peri-urban households with no grid connection or an unreliable one.
2. Small businesses such as shops, salons, barbers, butcheries and cyber cafés that lose money during power cuts.
3. Schools, clinics and churches that need steady lighting and power for equipment.

## What makes Kilele different

1. Every system is installed by a Kilele-certified technician, never a subcontractor.
2. All systems come with a remote monitoring unit, so faults are often spotted before the customer notices.
3. Customers can pay in full or through the Kilele Pay-As-You-Go (PAYG) plan using M-Pesa.
4. We have a service centre within 60 km of every customer we install for.

## Values

Honesty with customers, quality installation, and fast after-sales service. A Kilele salesperson never promises what the product cannot do.
