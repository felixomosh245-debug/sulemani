# The Kilele Sales Process

(Fictional, for testing only.)

## The five steps

1. Prospect: find customers through referrals, market days, chamas, churches and the leads list from the office.
2. Qualify: ask what the customer needs to power, how many hours a day, what they spend on energy today, and whether they prefer cash or PAYG.
3. Recommend: match the customer to one product. Do not show all three products at once; it confuses customers.
4. Close: complete the order form, collect the deposit through the Kilele M-Pesa Paybill only, and book the installation date.
5. Follow up: call the customer 7 days after installation to check they are happy, and ask for two referrals.

## Rules every salesperson must follow

1. Never collect cash. All deposits and payments go through the Kilele M-Pesa Paybill.
2. Never promise a delivery date more than 5 working days away without checking with the branch.
3. Always record every visit in the leads system the same day.
4. A Biz 1000 sale needs a site survey before the order is confirmed.

## Commission (for staff information)

Salespeople earn 4% of the cash price on each completed sale. For PAYG sales, the commission is paid once the customer has made 30 days of payments.

## Referral programme

A customer who refers a friend who buys gets 14 free days of PAYG credit.
