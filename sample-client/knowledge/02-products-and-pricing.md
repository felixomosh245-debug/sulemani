# Products and Pricing

(Fictional, for testing only.)

## Kilele Lite 50

1. Panel: 50 W. Battery: 12 Ah lithium.
2. Powers: 4 LED bulbs, phone charging, and a radio.
3. Typical runtime: 6 hours of lighting per night.
4. Cash price: KES 18,500.
5. PAYG: KES 2,000 deposit, then KES 45 per day for 12 months.
6. Warranty: 2 years on the battery, 5 years on the panel.

## Kilele Home 200

1. Panel: 200 W. Battery: 50 Ah lithium.
2. Powers: 8 LED bulbs, a 32-inch solar TV (included), phone charging, and a small fan.
3. Typical runtime: 5 hours of TV plus lighting per night.
4. Cash price: KES 64,000.
5. PAYG: KES 6,500 deposit, then KES 120 per day for 18 months.
6. Warranty: 3 years on the battery, 10 years on the panel, 1 year on the TV.

## Kilele Biz 1000

1. Panels: 4 x 250 W. Battery: 200 Ah lithium. Inverter: 1.5 kVA pure sine wave.
2. Powers: lighting, a fridge or freezer, clippers and hair dryers, a computer and printer.
3. Suitable for shops, salons, butcheries and small offices.
4. Cash price: KES 285,000.
5. PAYG: KES 30,000 deposit, then KES 520 per day for 24 months.
6. Warranty: 5 years on the battery and inverter, 10 years on the panels.
7. A site survey is required before a Biz 1000 quote is confirmed.

## Discounts and promotions

1. Cash buyers of the Home 200 or Biz 1000 get free installation (normally KES 3,500 and KES 12,000 respectively).
2. Lite 50 installation is always free.
3. Salespeople may not offer any other discount. Any special pricing must be approved in writing by the Branch Manager.

## PAYG rules

1. If a customer misses payments, the system locks after 3 days and unlocks as soon as they pay.
2. Customers can pay ahead at any time.
3. Ownership transfers to the customer once the last payment is made.
4. A customer can settle the balance early with a 10% discount on the remaining balance.
